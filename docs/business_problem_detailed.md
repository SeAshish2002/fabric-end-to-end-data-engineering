# Business Problem Statement

## Scenario: ShoppingMart Retail Analytics Platform

ShoppingMart is a mid-sized e-commerce and retail business experiencing rapid growth and increasing competition in the online marketplace.

The organization collects large volumes of structured and semi-structured data from multiple systems, including:

- Customer transactions
- Product catalogs
- Orders data
- Customer reviews
- Social media activity
- Web logs and engagement data

However, the company faces several challenges:

- Data exists in multiple formats and locations
- Lack of centralized analytics platform
- Difficulty tracking customer behavior
- Inconsistent reporting across departments
- Limited visibility into customer engagement trends
- Inability to generate business KPIs efficiently

---

# Project Objective

The goal of this project is to design and implement a scalable end-to-end modern data engineering solution using Microsoft Fabric and Medallion Architecture.

The solution aims to:

- Ingest structured and unstructured datasets
- Build metadata-driven and parameterized pipelines
- Store raw data in Bronze Lakehouse layer
- Clean and transform datasets in Silver layer using PySpark
- Generate business KPIs and aggregated datasets in Gold layer

---

# Business Goals

The project helps the business achieve:

- Centralized analytics architecture
- Improved data quality
- Faster reporting and insights
- Better customer behavior analysis
- Product performance monitoring
- Social sentiment tracking
- Website engagement analytics
- Scalable and reusable data pipelines

---

# Expected Outcomes

After implementation, ShoppingMart can:

- Track customer purchasing behavior
- Monitor engagement metrics
- Analyze social media sentiment
- Generate product-level KPIs
- Support data-driven decision making
